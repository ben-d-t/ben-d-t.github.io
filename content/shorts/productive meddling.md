What’s the difference between productive meddling and pointless meddling? 
- **Productive meddling is high-trust.** You probably need a lot of shared trust with the people you are meddling with. 
- **Productive meddling costs something**. It’s probably OK if they *sometimes* get annoyed at the meddling, but it’s not free. 
- **Productive meddling has a point.** Pointless meddling is unproductive — you should be able to say why you’re meddling if asked, and you should usually share. In general, [[communicate more|Choose to communicate more]].
