7 to 9pm each day. I’m surprised how much of my life comes down to what I do during these two hours. 
- How I feel during this time colors my impression of how the whole day went. 
- And I feel really different if I’m working, watching TV, reading a book, or coding. 
- The feeling builds over time (multiple nights working, or lots of TV) 

I think it’s because this is the window of time that is *most frequently free*.  

Between an office job, exercise, and life maintenance (cooking, cleaning, walking the dog), this is the window most frequently free fo me. 

Now my life is pretty simple, but I imagine it’s similar for many people, including parents. This window sets the tone: 
- Are you’re exhaustedly scrubbing dishes and folding laundry?
- Are you collapsing on the couch?
- Or is it a brief of window of time with your partner? 

And of course a shoutout to all the shift workers who don't have the luxury/curse of a stable schedule.
