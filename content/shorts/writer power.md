Why write at work? One good reason is that the person writing the doc has a lot of power. 

> The person who writes down the thing has tremendous power. Independent of their actual formal role... one of the ways you find the up and comers and a tech company, it's just like, who wrote down the plan? 
> [Marc Andreessen c/o How I Write podcast](https://youtu.be/tV6kDr1rwTs?si=o8Qmm5YTIiSN-2qu&t=2409)

Why is this? 

You get to *choose the anchor points of a first draft*. Because change is hard, people will bias towards keeping what you put down in a first draft. 

And if you're on the same page with leadership about the big picture, then *you get to fill in the details.* And some details really matter. 

Because writing clarifies and sharpens ideas, your ideas will be at the margin because they are written down, and people will assume so. 

P.S. [[feeling bored|Feeling bored is your fault]] — if you feel bored at work, consider writing a doc about a topic you keep hearing people *talk* about.
