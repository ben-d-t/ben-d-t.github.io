---
title: Focus on positive ambition AND contentment
alias: Focus on positive ambition AND contentment
date: 5/1/2024
---

I find I oscillate between feeling fired up and ambitious, to wanting to live on a quiet farm and tend a garden. 

It's easy to think the options are: 
Ambition <--- vs. ---> Contentment
But really there's positive/negative sides to each:

|          | Ambition                           | Contentment                                     |
| -------: | :--------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- |
| Positive | Excited to use ones gifts for good | Sustainable work, rest and fulfillment in truth |
| Negative | Selfish striving and stress        | Complacency and sloth; wasted potential         |

Two dangers: getting stuck in the negative row, but also having only one of the columns! It's hard to stay in the positive row without *both*.

So, I would like to focus on *both* positive ambition *and* positive contentment. 

That’s the line many people I admire walked.
