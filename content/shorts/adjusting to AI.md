What am *I* doing about AI and work? 
I’m reading a lot, thinking some, and writing a little. But what am I actually *doing* differently?

I am: 
1. Using the tools as often as possible and encouraging others too. See [[llms-at-work|How to try LLMs at work]]
2. Figuring out my taste. See [[figuring out taste|Why I want to figure out my taste]]
3. Working on my people-management and project-management skills (e.g., clear communication, describing a goal clearly, delegating effectively). See [The Knowledge Economy Is Over. Welcome to the Allocation Economy](https://every.to/chain-of-thought/the-knowledge-economy-is-over-welcome-to-the-allocation-economy)
4. Building a portfolio of [[content/projects|projects]], getting comfortable starting, testing, and evaluating lots of ideas. Important to see ideas through far enough! See [Tyler Cowen: Creativity, Talent & AI — futurework|life](https://www.futureworklife.com/listen/tyler-cowen-creativity-talent-ai)
5. Embracing flexibility in my career, and feeling increasingly confident about being a generalist. See [[chief-of-staff|Chief of Staff]] and also perhaps [Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Range:_Why_Generalists_Triumph_in_a_Specialized_World). 
6. Becoming relatively more interested in hardware and physical technology. See [Chip War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chip_War:_The_Fight_for_the_World%27s_Most_Critical_Technology)
