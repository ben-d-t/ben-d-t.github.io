title: Get agency by diving right in
alias: Get agency by diving right in
date: 6/14/2024
---
There’s much to be said for just rolling up your sleeves and getting your hands dirty. 

It seems to work both for learning something new (eg like a technical project) *and* for getting started on something you already know (eg reading a new book)

An example on my mind is building a simple GPT-wrapper chatbot for a favorite podcast. I was really excited about the idea, but it took me a while t go et going. The excuse I was making was wanting to have enough free time *in a row* to pull it off. But I think that was just an excuse. 

After getting stuck and unstuck a couple times, once the project was in a place to share I felt quite a sense of *agency*. 

My mental model shifted from “wow there’s so much going on that I don’t understand” to “wow this is exciting and I can learn & contribute.” 

Maybe this is totally obvious from the social science literature. But I was surprised how much agency I felt from just getting started and getting a bit messy with the early stage of learning.
