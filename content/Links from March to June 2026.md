1: [Dean Ball on the Ezra Klein show](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/why-the-pentagon-wants-to-destroy-anthropic/id1548604447) -- likely evergreen. By the way there will always be jobs if there is a human that can be sued for every agent action.

3: [Shyam Sankar on Invest Like The Best](https://overcast.fm/+ABA27sREpCQ) -- the maximal rate of learning is coincident with how much pain you are handling. Comforting career ladders don't maximize learning. 

4: [Jack Clark on Plain English](https://overcast.fm/+AA1LefZvrNo) & [Terence Tao on The Dwarkesh Podcast](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/terence-tao) -- is "ambient creativity" unique to humans? Researchers at the frontier consistently say you need time at rest to be creative. "You waste years by not being able to waste hours" etc. Creativity is another reason there may always be jobs. 

5: [What the Models Already Know: 67 Billion Tokens of Christian Moral Reasoning in the Pretraining Corpus](https://icmi-proceedings.com/ICMI-006-christian-tokens.html):
> Using a strict keyword classification methodology applied to a 100,000-document sample of The Pile — a foundational open-source pretraining corpus of approximately 825 billion tokens — we estimate that approximately 67 billion tokens (8.1% of the corpus) consist of explicitly Christian content: scripture, theology, sermons, catechisms, apologetics, and church history. This figure exceeds Islamic content by 32x, Buddhist content by 19x, Hindu content by 45x, and Jewish content by 93x. It is equivalent to 15 times the entire English Wikipedia, and exceeds the estimated total output of the AI safety research field by two to three orders of magnitude."

12: [the old world is dying by Jasmine Sun](https://jasmi.news/p/2026-advice)
> First, no nostalgia! The old world was bad. Those staid institutions and rigid hierarchies are not worth mourning. I went to a grinder high school and a grifter college. Both were mostly shitty and anti-human ways to live. Nothing makes you feel as compressed, quantized, and box-checked as AP season or the college application process. The notion that teens can smoke weed and skim Hegel for four years and have a cushy office job waiting on the other side was always doomed. Did new grad engineers have a God-given right to a $200k starting salary, while schoolteachers and paramedics grind for a fraction of the wage? There have to be better credentialing systems than one’s willingness to go into debt.

13: [Speed Can Reindustrialize America](https://austinvernon.substack.com/p/speed-can-reindustrialize-america) -- this essay is living rent free in my head. 

14: [Build, Baby, Build: How Housing Shapes Fertility by Benjamin Couillard](https://ti.org/pdfs/BuildBabyBuild.pdf) -- "To study the causal effect of rising housing costs on fertility, I vary them directly within the model, finding that rising costs since 1990 are responsible for 11% fewer children, 51% of the total fertility rate decline between the 2000s and 2010s, and 7 percentage points fewer young families in the 2010s."
