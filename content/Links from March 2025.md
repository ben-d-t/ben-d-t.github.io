7: [How to have good taste - by Henry Oliver](https://www.commonreader.co.uk/p/how-to-have-good-taste) -- "Believing that taste is a primarily personal question means believing that the canon is the canon because lots of people happen to prefer reading revenge tragedies and epic poems about Satan to reading anything else. It means believing that Chaucer is canonical because, much in the way some people prefer reading murder mysteries, many others prefer medieval stories told in iambic pentameter. Writers like Dante and Homer are not canonical because of a wide-spread personal taste for stories about the afterlife told in terza rima or because of a universal penchant for adventure stories with monsters told in hexameters. This is obviously false. The canon has been reapproved every generation because it is full of strange, unique, inventive, insightful work. It challenges us and shows us bigger things in life." ... "Some people don’t want good taste. Fine. Leave them be. There was never an easy way to have good taste. But today if you want to experience the heights of human accomplishment, you can. And much more easily than before. The only thing stopping you, is you."
