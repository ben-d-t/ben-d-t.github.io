14: [City upon a Hill - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_upon_a_Hill). Marilynne Robinson points out in *When I Was a Child I Read Books* that this phrase is often misconstrued; originally to John Winthrop (who’s quoting Jesus in the Sermon on the Mount) it meant that the Puritans mistakes would be visible for all to see. But over time it’s been re-read to be about American Exceptionalism, a *shining* city on a hill, with the adjective perhaps popularized by Reagan.
